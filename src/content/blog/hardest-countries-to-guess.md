Some Globle rounds fall in three guesses. Others drag past a dozen while you stab blindly at the
map. The difference is almost always the answer itself: a handful of countries are genuinely brutal
to pin down. This article ranks the **15 hardest countries to guess in Globle** and, more usefully,
explains *why* each is hard and how to crack it faster next time.

Our difficulty ranking blends three factors that make a country tough in a distance-based game like
Globle: **isolation** (few nearby neighbours to give warm clues), **small size** (a tiny target is
easy to skip past), and **cluster confusion** (sitting in a crowd of similar-looking neighbours).
Want to test these against real rounds? Spin up [Globle Unlimited](/) and see how many you can
name.

## Why some countries are so much harder

In Globle you find the answer by reading distance. That breaks down in three situations. **Isolated
island nations** have ocean on every side, so even a "close" guess can be thousands of kilometres
away. **Micro-states** are physically tiny, so the heat barely changes as you approach. And
**tightly packed clusters** light up several neighbours at once, leaving you to guess which of five
similar countries is the real target. The fifteen below are the worst offenders.

## The 15 hardest countries, ranked

### 1. Kiribati
A scattering of atolls straddling the equator and the date line in the central Pacific. Almost
nothing is near it, so every guess reads as far. Pure isolation.

### 2. Nauru
The world's smallest island nation, a single speck in the Pacific. Tiny and remote — the heatmap
gives you almost nothing to work with.

### 3. Tuvalu
Another minuscule Pacific state, easy to forget exists and easy to fly right past when guessing.

### 4. Maldives
A thread of low islands in the Indian Ocean. "Close" guesses like Sri Lanka or India are still a
long way off, so the colour stays misleadingly cool.

### 5. San Marino
A micro-state entirely surrounded by Italy. The heat around Italy spikes, but spotting San Marino
specifically takes a deliberate zoom.

### 6. Eswatini
A small landlocked kingdom tucked against South Africa and Mozambique. Easy to overlook in the
southern-African cluster.

### 7. Brunei
A small state on Borneo, hemmed in by Malaysia. Southeast Asia's island maze makes it a needle in a
haystack.

### 8. Bhutan
Wedged in the Himalayas between India and China. The giants around it grab your attention while
Bhutan hides in the gap.

### 9. Djibouti
Small and easy to lose in the busy Horn of Africa, surrounded by Eritrea, Ethiopia and Somalia.

### 10. Comoros
An island nation between Mozambique and Madagascar. Ocean isolation plus small size is a tough
combination.

### 11. Montenegro
One of several compact Balkan states. The whole region lights up together, so distinguishing
Montenegro from its neighbours is the challenge.

### 12. Timor-Leste
A young, small nation east of Indonesia. Frequently forgotten and easy to skip in the island sprawl.

### 13. Lesotho
A landlocked country entirely inside South Africa. If you are not thinking about enclaves, you will
miss it.

### 14. Vanuatu
A Pacific archipelago far from larger landmasses. Remote enough that distance clues stay weak.

### 15. Liechtenstein
A doubly landlocked micro-state between Switzerland and Austria. Tiny target, busy neighbourhood.

## Patterns in the hardest answers

Look down the list and the same themes repeat:

- **Pacific and Indian Ocean islands** dominate the top, because ocean isolation defeats distance
  clues.
- **Micro-states** like San Marino, Liechtenstein and Nauru are hard because the heat barely shifts
  as you near them.
- **Cluster countries** in the Balkans, the Horn of Africa and Southeast Asia hide in crowds of
  lookalikes.

## How to beat the hard ones

When a round will not crack, change tactics:

1. **Suspect the islands.** If your warmest guesses are still hundreds of kilometres out and the
   heat refuses to climb, the answer is probably an island nation. Start naming Pacific and Indian
   Ocean states.
2. **Zoom into hot clusters.** When several neighbours glow at once, stop guessing big countries and
   test the small ones between them, one by one.
3. **Remember the enclaves.** Lesotho inside South Africa, San Marino inside Italy, Vatican-style
   pockets — these only appear if you actively think of them.
4. **Use the kilometre reading.** On tiny targets the colour stalls, but the
   [closest-border distance](/how-to-play) keeps ticking down, so trust the number.

The reliable cure for all of this is exposure: the more countries you have actually guessed, the

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the hardest country to guess in Globle?**
Remote Pacific island nations like Kiribati, Nauru and Tuvalu are generally the hardest, because
ocean isolation means even nearby guesses read as very far away on the distance heatmap.

**How do you guess island countries in Globle?**
When your warmest guesses stay hundreds of kilometres out and the heat will not climb, switch to
naming island nations directly — start with the Pacific and Indian Ocean, then zoom in using the
kilometre distance rather than colour alone.
